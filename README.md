# Mathematics-3: Linear Algebra Interactive Laboratory
### Newton School of Technology · Batch 2026–27

An interactive, visual, and conceptual companion laboratory for 3rd-semester students studying **Linear Algebra (Mathematics-3)**.

Designed to accompany `LA_Notebook_1.ipynb`, bridging pure algebraic notation and visual geometry through real-time interactive simulations, 3D rotatable spaces, acoustic wave superposition, and digital pixel processing.

---

## 🌟 Features

- **Zero-Dependency & Browser-Native:** Runs 100% in any browser without requiring local Python environments, Jupyter servers, or command-line setups.
- **Module 1: System of Linear Equations**
  - Linearity rate of change ($\Delta y / \Delta x$) invariant simulations.
  - 2D geometric intersections and full solvability parameter studio.
  - Theorem 1 Visual Matrix: The Three States in $\mathbb{R}^2$.
  - 3D Rotatable Planes Dashboard: All 6 intersection configurations in $\mathbb{R}^3$.
  - The Row-Verse vs. Column-Verse dual visualization.
- **Module 2: Matrix Multiplication**
  - Additive RGB light synthesis & acoustic wave superposition studio.
  - The Order Paradox & inner dimension compatibility checker.
  - The Mul-Tea-Plication Kitchen (Column-Way tea recipes).
  - Binary Images as Matrices (Left multiplier $P$ vs. Right multiplier $Q$).
  - Menu Matrix $AB$: The Canteen Menu studio & the domain isomorphism table.
  - The Invariant Proof: Why permutation matrices cannot create new shapes.
  - $8 \times 8$ Image Transformation studio.
- **Module 3: Vectors and their Properties**
  - The Three Perspectives: Physics (Arrow), CS (Array), Math (Space).
  - Head-to-Tail Rule vs. Parallelogram Law geometry.
  - 2D Continuous Span explorer & 1D line trap.
  - Rotatable 3D Subspace Laboratory ($\mathbb{R}^3$).
  - The Linear Alchemist Capstone Game.
- **Built-in Student Utilities:**
  - 🧮 **Interactive Matrix Sandbox & Solver:** Solves $2 \times 2$ and $3 \times 3$ systems with rank diagnosis and 3D visualization.
  - 📐 **Exam Formula Reference Sheet:** High-yield KaTeX summary of core theorems and axioms.
  - 🗂️ **Active Recall 3D Flashcards:** 10 review flip cards with self-scoring mastery tracker.
  - 🔍 **Universal Search:** Instant search across all 22 labs and 94 notebook cells.
  - 🌙/☀️ **Dark Mode Toggle:** Universal theme persistence.
